# Code of conduct

gaitkeeper is a small technical project, and we want it to be a place where anyone can
ask a question, report a problem, or disagree about a result without being made to feel
unwelcome.

## What we expect

* Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to the field or the tools.
* Criticize ideas, code and results, not people.
* Assume good faith, and ask before assuming.
* Credit other people's work, including the policies, models and benchmarks this project
  tests against.
* Accept a maintainer's decision on scope, and say so openly if you disagree.

## What is not acceptable

* Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in public or in private.
* Discriminatory jokes or language, or unwelcome sexual attention.
* Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
* Sustained disruption of issues, discussions or reviews.

## Scope

This applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and anywhere
someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainer, Dundy Pasupuleti, at dundysm@gmail.com. Reports are
kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, lock threads, or block someone from
the project, and will tell the person reported what was done and why, unless that would put
the reporter at risk.

This code of conduct is adapted in spirit from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
